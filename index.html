About me

Hello my name is Douglas,


I have been mapping since January 2024, slowly gaining experience with each project. I started with small, simple maps and have since progressed to larger, more complex projects. My focus is on optimization and creating surreal, immersive environments. I enjoy the entire process of map creation, from initial concept to final compile, and am always looking for ways to improve my skills.


Thank you for viewing my portfolio!


Mapping Process

1. Brush Layout Initial layout of brushes, ensuring they are being used efficiently.
2. Textures Applying brush textures to each face and and adding overlays/decals.
3. Props Placement of props and any map related logic.
4. Lighting Creating a specific atmosphere and fixing any lighting issues.
5. Optimization Cutting visleafs using hint/skips, adding area portals, creating func_detail/propper entities, etc.
